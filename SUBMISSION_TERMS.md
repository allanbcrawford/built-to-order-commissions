# Submission terms

> Draft. Have a lawyer read this before the channel launches. It follows the pattern in the research doc (Tom Scott's terms, and the Greer v. Carlson risk).

When you submit a commission to Built to Order, you agree to these terms:

1. **No payment.** You will not get money for your commission, now or later, even if the build earns money.
2. **Credit.** If your commission is built, you get credit in the video and in the repo, in the form you ask for.
3. **Licence, not ownership.** You keep your idea. You give the channel a free, permanent licence to build it, show it, and publish it.
4. **Open source.** Code is published under Apache 2.0. Hardware plans are published under CERN-OHL.
5. **No promise to build.** The channel picks which commissions to build. A vote result is advice, not a promise. Allan can veto any commission.
6. **Not a contest.** There is no prize. You do not need to like, subscribe, or pay to submit or to vote. A shipped build is a thank-you, not a prize.
7. **Hosted versions.** A hosted version is only for people on the beta waitlist, for a set term. After the term, the code stays open source.
8. **Hardware.** Hardware ships to the US only. Allan funds small builds. Larger builds are funded up front by the person who suggested them, or by a pre-sale or Kickstarter after a prototype video.
9. **Your idea is your own.** Do not submit ideas that you know belong to someone else, or that are confidential.
